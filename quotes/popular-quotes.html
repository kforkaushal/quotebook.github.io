What makes a quote "popular"?

Popular quotes are lines that have been widely shared, cited in books, films, speeches, and social media across multiple decades and cultures. They resonate because they distill a universal human truth into a single memorable sentence — lines from Einstein, Maya Angelou, Gandhi, and Shakespeare consistently rank among the most shared of all time.
