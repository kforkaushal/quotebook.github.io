Frequently Asked Questions

What is Gandhi Jayanti and why is it celebrated?

Gandhi Jayanti is observed across India on 2nd October each year to honor the birth of Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi (1869–1948). Renowned worldwide as the pioneer of Satyagraha (resistance through mass non-violent civil disobedience), his philosophy led India to freedom and inspired civil rights movements across the globe. Internationally, the UN marks this day as the International Day of Non-Violence.

What are the three main pillars of Gandhi's philosophy?

The three core pillars of Gandhi's teachings are Satya (uncompromising commitment to Truth), Ahimsa (active non-violence and love toward all living beings), and Sarvodaya (progress and upliftment of everyone, especially the most vulnerable).
